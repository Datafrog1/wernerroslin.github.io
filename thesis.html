THESIS CASE · WERNERROSLIN.FI

Developing a digital personal brand with generative AI.

This website is the practical case behind my thesis: how a personal website can support professional visibility, personal branding and a coherent digital identity — and where generative AI can help in the process.

DEVELOPMENT QUESTION

From “I built a website” to “I built a digital brand platform”

How can a personal website be used to build a digital personal brand, and how can generative AI support that process?

The case shifts the focus from technology alone to business and marketing: target audience, value proposition, content, credibility, user journey and measurable digital quality.

FRAMEWORK

Four layers of the case

01

Brand

Who am I professionally, what do I want to be known for, and is the message consistent?

02

Audience

What should a recruiter, collaborator or professional contact understand quickly?

03

Experience

Can visitors find the right information quickly on desktop and mobile?

04

Do accessibility, performance and SEO support the credibility of the site?

GENERATIVE AI

Where AI helps — and where it does not replace judgement

Useful for

  • Generating structure and alternatives
  • Drafting text and code
  • Explaining unfamiliar concepts
  • Creating test and review checklists
  • Speeding up repetitive tasks

Needs human review

  • Brand voice and authenticity
  • Correctness of generated code
  • Accessibility decisions
  • Relevance to the real target audience
  • Claims about results and performance

DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

An iterative build–test–improve cycle

  1. Define

    Clarify target audience, brand message, content and goals.

  2. Build

    Create the site with lightweight HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

  3. Use AI

    Ask for ideas, drafts and technical options — not final truth.

  4. Validate

    Review content manually and test usability, accessibility and performance.

  5. Improve

    Keep what works, remove what does not, and document the learning.

OUTCOME

What the case demonstrates

A personal website can function as a controlled marketing channel: it brings professional identity, projects, content and contact paths together without depending entirely on a third-party platform.

The practical lesson from the AI-assisted process is equally important: AI is most valuable when it accelerates exploration and routine work, while the user remains responsible for relevance, accuracy, brand voice and quality.
